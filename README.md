## My Favorite Series/Movie

One of my favorite series is *The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya*
Some of the reasons this is one of my favorite series is how it **presents** itself. It is a slice-of-life highschool anime, with supernatural elements to it that makes this series more interesting. For me though, the thing that makes this series truly unique are the *dialogue* and how they do it. The tone that is has is, in my opinion, quite unique even today where you can say that some series has a similar vibe to it like *Seishun Buta*. Even then, I haven't seen another series that quite match the vibe that the *Haruhi Series* has that just hits right with me. There are other aspects of this series(anime) that make it a great series, such as:
> Interesting characters

> Unreliable Narrator utilised nicely that shows the main character's traits well

> Great direction

> Great animation

> Intruiging mystery

> Meta storytelling through broadcasting episodes chronologically out of order, redoing episodes to emphasize a time loop(though this one is quite controversial)

And others that make up how good the series is.
